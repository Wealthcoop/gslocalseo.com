# Master Ledger: High-LCV & Underserved Local SEO Niches

This ledger documents high-value, underserved local industries specifically engineered for automated lead acquisition, Google Maps 3-Pack capture, and high-conversion cold outreach.

---

## The Evaluation Criteria for "Gold Standard" Niches

Every niche in this ledger satisfies five mandatory operational hurdles:
1. **High Ticket / High LCV**: Minimum \$2,500 ticket size or \$5,000+ recurring contract value.
2. **Low SEO Sophistication**: Dominated by technicians, craftsmen, or solo operators without dedicated agencies.
3. **High Proximity Search Intent**: Homeowners and businesses search on Google Maps for verified local specialists within 10–20 miles.
4. **Frictionless ROI Math**: Closing just **one** single customer from Google Maps pays for Gold Standard SEO services for an entire year.
5. **Clear GBP Category Mismatch**: High prevalence of competitors using incorrect or generic categories (e.g., "Contractor" instead of "Waterproofing Service").
